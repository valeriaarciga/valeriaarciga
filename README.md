- 🔬 **Research** in operations research and spatial optimization with Dr. Fernando Elizalde Ramírez (Tec de Monterrey): location–routing, retail site selection, multi-objective stochastic optimization for reforestation, and school timetabling.
- 🏦 **Risk Models Intern** applying causal inference, machine learning and prescriptive AI to credit decisions.

## 🧩 Selected projects

| Project | What it does | Methods |
|---|---|---|
| 🌵 [**reforestation-species-allocation**](https://github.com/valeriaarciga/reforestation-species-allocation) | Assigns 10 native semi-arid species to a hexagonal planting grid under uncertain pre-existing vegetation, trading off competition vs. monoculture. Presented at ENOAN 2026. | MILP (Gurobi, ε-constraint), NSGA-II, Monte Carlo |
| 🚚 [**smio-clrp-solver**](https://github.com/marifercc/-smio-clrp-solver) · [**smio-lrp-challenge-2026**](https://github.com/valeriaarciga/smio-lrp-challenge-2026) | Capacitated location–routing solver that won 1st place (undergraduate) in the SMIO–Hexaly challenge, on instances of up to 3,000 customers and 50 depots. | Greedy construction, local search, LNS, PyVRP |
| 🏪 [**retail-location-oxxo**](https://github.com/valeriaarciga/retail-location-oxxo) | Chooses convenience-store locations in Monterrey on the real street network, accounting for competition and cannibalization. Precursor to the Springer chapter. | MIP (PuLP / GAMSPy), OSMnx, GeoPandas |
| 🛒 [**oxxo-planogram-optimization**](https://github.com/valeriaarciga/oxxo-planogram-optimization) | Places products on store shelves to maximize visibility while respecting physical space, with an interactive dashboard. | MILP (Gurobi), FFD bin packing, 2-opt, Streamlit |
| ❄️ [**smart-cooler-failure-prediction**](https://github.com/valeriaarciga/smart-cooler-failure-prediction) | Predicts next-month failures of connected beverage coolers from sensor data and plans service visits. | XGBoost, CRISP-DM, PuLP |
| 📊 [**poverty-prediction-mexico**](https://github.com/valeriaarciga/poverty-prediction-mexico) | Predicts CONEVAL multidimensional poverty indicators for Mexican municipalities from 2020 census data. | 10 regression models, grid search + CV (test R² 0.82) |
